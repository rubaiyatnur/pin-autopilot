Toolvai Pin Autopilot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

What this tool is

Toolvai Pin Autopilot is a personal tool used by one person, Rubaiyat Nur, to publish pins for their own digital products (images, titles, descriptions and product links) to their own Pinterest boards. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we collect

None from visitors or other Pinterest users. The tool does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about anyone other than its owner. It reads only the owner's own product pages and the owner's own Pinterest boards.

Data we store

The access token for the owner's Pinterest account is stored only on the owner's own computer. Lists of products and pins already created are stored in local files on that computer. Nothing is sent to any third party other than Pinterest, to publish the owner's own pins.

Cookies and tracking

This page and the tool use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Because no personal data about other people is collected, there is nothing to access or delete. The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in their Pinterest account settings.
